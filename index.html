Research Boundaries

Available in this app

HTTPS request timings, browser connectivity, exposed network estimates, local experiment records, charts, statistical summaries, and CSV/JSON exports.

Requires additional hardware or software

Actual Wi-Fi signal strength, CSI, raw radio samples, frequency spectra, and detailed channel measurements require appropriate device or operating-system access.

Not inferred by this app

The presence of a person, their location, movements through walls, private communications, or the physical cause of a network anomaly.
